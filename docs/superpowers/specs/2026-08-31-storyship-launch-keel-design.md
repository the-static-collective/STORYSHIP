# STORYSHIP — Launch Keel / Provenance Lift

**Status:** approved architectural design / repository founding constitution

**Date:** 2026-08-31

**Repository:** `the-static-collective/STORYSHIP`

**Launch posture:** **Provenance Lift**

**Motto:** **The story is true; the ship is how it travels.**

## 1. Decision

`the-static-collective/STORYSHIP` becomes the canonical repository for the executable voyage layer previously designed inside Haunted Phonography.

This is not a rewrite of history and not a declaration that STORYSHIP was always an independent repository.

The project has a documented formation road:

```text
Autodiscography / live generator history
        ↓
Haunted Phonography STORYSHIP design + amendments
        ↓
no-spend continuity-spine prototype
        ↓
2026-08-31 human launch decision
        ↓
the-static-collective/STORYSHIP
```

The new repository owns the voyage going forward while preserving exact references to the worlds in which its laws and first implementation experiments were formed.

The governing migration law is:

> **Move ownership forward. Do not move origin backward.**

## 2. Why lift instead of copy or finish in Haunted Phonography

Three paths were available.

### A. Finish STORYSHIP inside Haunted Phonography

This preserves repository locality but leaves the flagship voyage as a subsystem of its destination. That weakens the already-established customs boundary.

### B. Copy the Storyship files into the empty repository and call them native

This produces the fastest apparent launch but creates false provenance, duplicated authority, and ambiguous constitutional history.

### C. Provenance Lift — adopted

Give STORYSHIP its own executable body while binding imported laws and prototype code to exact source receipts.

This keeps the strongest existing boundary:

```text
STORYSHIP owns voyage
Haunted Phonography owns customs
Vault owns raw historical evidence
human owns attributable steering decisions
provider owns provider-side generation facts
```

No owner gains another owner's authority merely because the voyage links them.

## 4. Repository authority boundary

### Autodiscography Vault

Owns preserved raw historical evidence and provider-facing provenance.

STORYSHIP references Vault cuts. It does not rewrite Vault evidence into cleaner narrative history.

### STORYSHIP

Owns:

- its ordered constitution receipt;
- voyage events;
- Storyship-local branch ancestry;
- deterministic replay projections;
- sealed transfer packets;
- crossing receipts;
- actual credit accounting recorded from observation;
- human encounter and steering receipts;
- candidate arrival records;
- voyage-local unresolved possibility.

### Provider / Suno

Owns externally observable provider facts about generation.

STORYSHIP may record observed provider results. It does not claim hidden model identity, memory, intent, determinism, or continuity the provider does not expose.

### Human operator

Owns attributable:

- supplied inputs;
- encounters;
- selections;
- refusals;
- returns;
- continuation choices;
- explicit interpretations;
- the final GO decision for an exact launch packet.

Human testimony is evidence of encounter and decision, not proof of hidden provider state.

### Haunted Phonography

Owns destination customs:

- admission;
- refusal;
- influence-only handling;
- descendant/native status.

```text
arrival != admission
```

STORYSHIP may link a later Haunted Phonography customs receipt. It cannot manufacture one.

### 3rdi

May render observer-local projections and expose what was available, focused, and known-at a cut.

3rdi is an eye, not a hand. A projection cannot mutate voyage history or decide customs.

### ALEX

May research, pressure-test, and classify claims and counterexamples.

ALEX findings can become attributable source cuts or research receipts. Research does not gain runtime authority by recurrence or confidence.

### LOADOUT

May bind capabilities needed for a run. Loading a capability does not expand constitutional authority.

### GitBook / Daily Slice / Novelist / narrative surfaces

May preserve orientation, story, re-entry aids, and historical witness.

They may interpret the voyage. They do not become the canonical event road.

## 5. Core dependency DAG

The executable dependency direction is:

```text
RAW OWNER EVIDENCE
        ↓
STORYSHIP VOYAGE EVENT
        ↓
DETERMINISTIC PROJECTION
        ↓
SEALED TRANSFER PACKET
        ↓
EXTERNAL GENERATION OBSERVATION
        ↓
HUMAN ENCOUNTER / STEERING
        ↓
CANDIDATE ARRIVAL
        ↓
HAUNTED PHONOGRAPHY CUSTOMS
```

The graph must remain acyclic for authority-bearing transitions.

Allowed later interpretation may point backward as a **reference**, never as a mutation.

Hard invariant:

> **No downstream state writes backward into its evidence ancestry.**

Hard branch invariant:

> **No two worldlines collapse merely because their current renderings match.**

## 6. Canonical history and projections

The canonical Storyship history is an append-only ordered event ledger.

Accepted events are never edited to make later interpretation look ancestral.

Correction, contradiction, supersession, refusal, cooling, and reinterpretation append new events.

At an exact event cut, deterministic projectors derive:

```text
REALITY
NARRATIVE
MANIFEST
OPEN BERTH
BRANCH HEADS
DORMANT BRANCHES
RELATIONSHIP THREADS
```

`MEMORY` is packet-facing derived state. It is not an independent mutable database.

Earlier cuts remain addressable after later events are appended.

Timestamp recency never decides branch currentness.

## 7. Reality / Narrative law

Every voyage may carry narrative meaning without sacrificing historical truth.

```text
reality occurrence          <-> narrative interpretation
pair generated              <-> twin birth
branch selected             <-> one takes the wheel
unselected sibling          <-> ghost aboard
model behavior shift        <-> weather changes
candidate reaches customs   <-> reaches port
customs refusal             <-> remains offshore
```

But:

```text
interpretation != observation
recognition    != evidence of hidden identity
resemblance    != ancestry
selection      != deletion
arrival        != admission
story truth    != runtime authority
```

Narrative must preserve `basis_event_ids` sufficient to show what real road it interprets.

## 8. Native twin birth and branch law

A provider generation that returns two sibling artifacts creates two attributable branches from one request state.

A sibling not selected for immediate continuation remains historical.

```text
not selected != deleted
```

A branch may be:

```text
live
dormant
stopped
```

A later descendant from dormant history appends a new edge; it does not rewrite the earlier steering decision.

Branch composition requires explicit named parents and an admitted composition rule.

If multiple live branch heads exist and no steering/composition event resolves them, currentness is `unresolved`.

## 9. Historical self-selection gate

The original STORYSHIP design requires recovery of the pre-Bandcamp self-selecting / self steering practice.

Launch implementation must not substitute:

```text
pick the better song
highest score
latest artifact
most similar artifact
model ranking
```

for that historical mechanism.

The recovered mechanism must be documented as an attributable human practice with:

- source testimony or source evidence;
- allowed outcomes;
- what information the selector actually had;
- how non-selected siblings were treated;
- uncertainty where memory or evidence is incomplete.

If the historical mechanism cannot be recovered exactly, the launch must say what portion is recovered and what remains unresolved. It may not fabricate certainty.

## 10. OPEN BERTH

OPEN BERTH is deliberately unresolved possibility that the current crossing is permitted to develop.

It is not a generic null bucket.

These may not be mapped into OPEN BERTH:

```text
missing raw evidence
inaccessible source
protected silence
explicit refusal
known prohibition
forgotten metadata
unresolved branch ownership
```

An Open Berth record must carry:

```text
question_or_possibility
basis_event_ids[]
allowed_scope
explicit_non_imports[]
opened_by
status
```

A protected-silence-to-open-berth mapping must fail closed.

## 13. Human-mediated provider crossing

The first launch must not depend on undocumented Suno APIs, hidden endpoints, reusable auth material, or a provider SDK that does not exist.

The viable v0 crossing is explicitly human-mediated:

```text
storyship seal packet
        ↓
human carries declared generation input to provider
        ↓
provider generates observable sibling pair
        ↓
human / ordinary user-accessible evidence records provider result
        ↓
storyship append generation-observed
        ↓
human encounter + historical steering
        ↓
storyship append consequence
```

This is not a deficiency in the evidence model. The human is already part of the relationship passenger and remains the strongest continuous participant across substrate changes.

Provider observations must keep exposed provider fields distinct from human interpretation.

No credential or reusable session token enters the repository.

## 16. No-spend continuity crucible

Before live flagship spend, the new repo must pass the inherited eleven-case crucible.

1. **Replay and re-entry** — same events, same environment, fresh process: identical declared identities/bytes.
2. **Stale cut** — later append does not mutate old packet or projections.
3. **Twin branch preservation** — one sibling can go dormant and later return without rewriting selection history.
4. **Ambiguous heads** — unresolved stays unresolved rather than choosing newest.
5. **History reinterpretation** — new projector preserves old projection and appends derivation receipt.
6. **Protected-silence attack** — cannot map protected silence into OPEN BERTH.
7. **Narrative overwrite attack** — cannot replace raw observation with cleaner interpretation.
8. **Relationship/carrier confusion** — lookalike artifact without attributable exchange history does not establish lineage.
9. **Abstraction counterexample** — failed observation method does not silently refute continuity law.
10. **Customs boundary** — arrival without HP receipt remains unadmitted.
11. **Tamper evidence** — changed parent, receipt, constitution, or source cut fails closed.

The crucible may return:

```text
supports
refuses
unresolved
```

against a typed target. It does not certify its own abstraction.

## 18. Preflight sequence

The shortest honest road from empty repository to DEPARTURE is:

```text
0. found repository constitution + ancestry receipt
1. port canonicalization compatibility witness
2. port / native-ize contract + ledger with RED/GREEN preservation
3. implement deterministic replay + branch DAG
4. implement MEMORY / OPEN BERTH + Transfer Packet machinery
5. implement typed claim / result / customs-link contracts
6. execute eleven-case fixture-only continuity crucible
7. prove fresh-process re-entry + fail-closed verification
8. recover and bind historical self-selection mechanism
9. bind one immutable/raw Vault source cut
10. seal Transfer Packet 000
11. seal actual protected late-voyage credit reserve
12. obtain explicit human GO bound to exact repository head + Packet 000 identity
13. DEPARTURE
```

Step 12 is intentionally late.

No earlier enthusiasm, approval, conversation, spec adoption, or repository creation authorizes an as-yet-uncreated packet identity.

## 19. Protected late reserve

Live budget is tracked in actual observed credit debit, not inferred song count.

The original working allocation remains a policy input, not a guessed fact:

```text
MAIN STORYSHIP: approximately 95%
DDD SIDECAR:    up to approximately 5% / 500 credits
```

Before DEPARTURE, a launch receipt must bind the actual available-credit observation and a protected minimum reserve for `APPROACH → CUSTOMS`.

Unused DDD capacity may return to the flagship.

The ship must be able to stop early without consuming the protected reserve.

## 20. Failure posture

The implementation fails closed when:

- constitution ancestry cannot be resolved;
- event or receipt identity verification fails;
- source cuts are ambiguous;
- earlier cuts mutate after later append;
- branch currentness is guessed;
- narrative attempts to overwrite evidence;
- protected silence leaks into OPEN BERTH;
- artifact similarity is promoted into relationship ancestry;
- imported HP prototype records are relabeled as native Storyship history;
- provider observation cannot be distinguished from human interpretation;
- actual credit debit cannot be accounted for;
- a customs result is claimed without an HP-owned receipt;
- a machine third-arm verdict is attempted before its crucible;
- live spend is attempted before exact-head + Packet 000 human GO.

Failure is itself recordable voyage history when an accepted voyage already exists. It is not cleanup noise.

## 22. Testing discipline

Implementation follows RED → GREEN per behavioral slice.

Every ported behavior must first have a test that demonstrates the intended native boundary.

The old green HP prototype is evidence that Tasks 1–2 were viable in their original environment. It is not permission to skip native tests after changing repository, imports, schemas, or canonicalization policy.

Tests should privilege invariants over implementation shape:

```text
same declared history -> same projection
new event             -> old cut unchanged
same rendering        -/-> same worldline
reference backward    -/-> mutation backward
arrival               -/-> admission
selection             -/-> deletion
unknown               -/-> open berth
loaded capability     -/-> expanded authority
```

## 23. Launch UX

The first usable experience should optimize for **operator legibility**, not polish.

A human should be able to answer, at any point:

```text
WHERE AM I?
which voyage / branch / cut

WHAT IS TRUE?
Reality projection + source receipts

WHAT ARE WE CARRYING?
Memory / Manifest projection

Open Berth only
```

The CLI should print IDs and source refs prominently enough that receipts can be copied without opening internal code.

A graphical dashboard may come later as a 3rdi projection. It is not a launch dependency.

## 24. Success criterion

The minimum launch is successful when a fresh process can reconstruct and verify one complete no-spend voyage from source cut through packet, twin branches, human encounter/steering, dormant residue, candidate arrival, and an unadmitted customs boundary — then produce the exact same sealed artifacts from the exact same history.

The live voyage succeeds at a higher level when:

> **something recognizably descended from the old world can continue producing new locally admitted consequence after the originating runtime is gone, while ancestor, carrier, relationship, descendant, and authority remain distinguishable.**
